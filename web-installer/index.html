Lakatos OS — WebSerial Installer

Zero-install browser flasher for the Device

Before You Begin

  1. Open this page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on Windows, macOS, or Linux.
  2. Plug the Device into your computer with a data-capable USB cable.
  3. Click Install below and select the USB serial port (typically USB Serial, CH340, CP2102, ttyUSB0, or cu.usbserial-*).

Install Lakatos OS

Creates the flash partitions on the Device, installs the bootloader, Lakatos OS kernel, and OTA updater, initializes the default Device settings, and formats the /flash LittleFS filesystem.

Your browser does not support WebSerial. Please use Chrome or Edge. WebSerial requires HTTPS or localhost.

After installation completes, tap the triangle at the top right on the Device and open Config to connect to Wi-Fi.
